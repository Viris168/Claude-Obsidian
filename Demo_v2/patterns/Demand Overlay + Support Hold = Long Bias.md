# Pattern: Demand Overlay + Support Hold = Long Bias

## Description
This pattern appears when strong demand is visible in the order book, order book depth delta is highly positive, and price holds a key support level instead of breaking down.

## Typical Signals
- Strong demand overlay
- Positive order book depth delta
- Price holding key support
- Buyers defend the level
- Price starts pushing up after holding

## Interpretation
This usually suggests real bid support under price. If the level keeps holding and demand does not disappear, the market is more likely to bounce or continue upward from that zone.

## Trade Logic
- If demand overlay stays strong and depth delta remains positive, prefer long
- Wait for price to hold and react upward
- Avoid long if support breaks or demand vanishes

## Invalidation
- Depth delta weakens sharply
- Demand overlay disappears
- Price loses the support zone and cannot reclaim it
