# Pattern: Short Squeeze Continuation

## Core Idea
Overextended short positioning gets forcibly closed, fueling a sharp continuation move upward.

## Description
When a large number of shorts are trapped at a key level, a break above resistance triggers cascading liquidations. Each short cover pushes price higher, which triggers more liquidations. The move feeds itself. This pattern is strongest when funding is deeply negative or neutral, OI is elevated, and the break above resistance is supported by rising CVD.

---

## Required Conditions
- High short OI or negative/neutral funding (shorts are crowded)
- Price breaks above a clear resistance level
- CVD rising during the break (buyers absorbing and adding, not just shorts covering)
- Liquidation heatmap shows a cluster above price that gets swept

## Supporting Signals
- Low TRL (retail not positioned long, shorts exposed)
- Spot volume increase on the breakout candle
- Orderbook imbalance shifts positive after the break
- Price closes above resistance, not just wicks above

## Invalid / Weak Version
- Weak when funding is already extremely positive before the break (longs crowded, not shorts)
- Weak when CVD is falling during the break (distribution, not a squeeze)
- Weak when OI drops sharply during the move (shorts covering without new longs adding)
- Invalid when price fails to hold above the broken resistance level

---

## Failure Conditions
- Price breaks above resistance but immediately falls back below it
- CVD diverges and continues lower after the break
- OI collapses — shorts closed but no new longs entered

## What Usually Happens Next
Price accelerates through the liquidity cluster above, pauses briefly as shorts finish covering, then either consolidates or continues if longs are also entering with conviction.

## What Confirms Entry
- Break and close above resistance
- CVD rising or holding during the break
- OI holding or increasing (new longs entering, not just shorts covering)

## What Cancels the Idea
- Price reclaims below the broken resistance
- CVD drops sharply after the initial break
- Funding flips extremely positive immediately (longs now crowded)
