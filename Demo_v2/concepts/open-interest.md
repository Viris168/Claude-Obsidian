# Open Interest (OI)

## Definition
The total number of outstanding derivative contracts (futures/options) that have not been settled.

## Why It Matters
- Rising OI + rising price = bullish conviction (new longs entering)
- Rising OI + falling price = bearish conviction (new shorts entering)
- Falling OI + price move = position unwinding (less conviction)

## Key Signals
- OI spike before a move = fuel for continuation or squeeze
- OI drop after a move = exhaustion / potential reversal
