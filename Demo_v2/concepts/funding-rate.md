# Funding Rate

## Definition
A periodic payment between long and short traders in perpetual futures to keep the contract price anchored to spot.

## Reading It
- Positive funding → longs pay shorts → market is long-biased
- Negative funding → shorts pay longs → market is short-biased
- Extreme positive = overheated longs, potential long squeeze
- Extreme negative = overheated shorts, potential short squeeze

## Key Signals
- Sustained high positive funding during uptrend = warning of correction
- Deeply negative funding with price at support = short squeeze setup
