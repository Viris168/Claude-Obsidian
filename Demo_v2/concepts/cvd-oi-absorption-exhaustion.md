# CVD + OI Absorption and Exhaustion

## Core Idea
Price, CVD, and Open Interest should be read together. When they diverge, they can show absorption or exhaustion before price fully confirms the move.

## Absorption

### Bearish Absorption
- Price makes lower highs or fails to push higher
- CVD rises
- OI rises
- Interpretation: Aggressive buyers are entering, but price is not moving higher. Sellers may be absorbing buy pressure.
- Bias: Bearish if price loses structure after this behavior

### Bullish Absorption
- Price makes higher lows or stops falling
- CVD falls
- OI rises
- Interpretation: Aggressive sellers are entering, but price is not moving lower. Buyers may be absorbing sell pressure.
- Bias: Bullish if price reclaims structure after this behavior

## Exhaustion

### Buyers Exhaustion
- Price pushes higher
- CVD makes lower highs or trends down
- OI falls
- Interpretation: Price is rising while buying pressure and participation weaken. Upside may be running out of fuel.
- Bias: Bearish reversal risk if price fails to hold structure

### Sellers Exhaustion
- Price pushes lower
- CVD makes higher lows or trends up
- OI falls
- Interpretation: Price is falling while selling pressure and participation weaken. Downside may be running out of fuel.
- Bias: Bullish reversal risk if price reclaims structure

## Important Rules
- Do not use CVD or OI alone.
- Always combine with price structure.
- Absorption is stronger near support, resistance, liquidity zones, or range extremes.
- Exhaustion needs confirmation from reclaim, breakdown failure, or structure shift.
- These are probability signals, not guarantees.
