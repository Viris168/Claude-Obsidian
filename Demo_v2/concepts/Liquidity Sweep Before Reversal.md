## Market Maker / Reversal Behavior

- Market makers often do not leave nearby liquidation clusters untouched before a major reversal.
- If a large liquidation cluster is visible near price, the market may sweep that area first before reversing.
- A reversal is more reliable after liquidity is taken and price reclaims the swept level.
- Do not assume reversal before checking whether nearby liquidation heatmap liquidity has already been cleared.
