# CME Gap

## Definition
A CME gap forms when Bitcoin opens on CME futures at a different price from where it closed before the weekend or another market break.

## Reading It
- CME gaps often act as liquidity magnets.
- Price frequently revisits CME gap zones after they form.
- Many CME gaps are filled within the following week, but this is a tendency, not a guarantee.
- The fill probability is stronger when the gap aligns with nearby liquidity, weak structure, or a broader mean-reversion setup.

## Key Signals
- Gap is close to current price
- Gap aligns with visible liquidity on the heatmap
- Price rejects away from continuation levels and starts rotating toward the gap
- OI, CVD, and volume support movement toward the gap

## Risk Notes
- Not every CME gap fills quickly.
- Strong trend conditions can leave gaps open longer than expected.
- Do not trade toward the gap by itself without structure, liquidity, and confirmation.
- A catalyst can override the gap-fill tendency.
