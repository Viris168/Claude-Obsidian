# Pattern: Range High Fakeout + Short Re-Accumulation

## Core Idea
Repeated fakeouts above a range high with declining volume suggest buy-side liquidity is being absorbed and larger participants may be building short exposure, not confirming a sustainable bullish reversal.

## Description
Strong bottoms after prolonged downtrends usually take time to form. They are processes, not single events. Large players need liquidity, time, and trapped participants to build meaningful positions without moving price against themselves.

In a true bottoming process, price often sweeps range lows multiple times, absorbs sell-side liquidity, reclaims the range, and shows seller exhaustion through declining volume on each sweep.

This setup is the opposite: price repeatedly pushes above the range high, fails to hold, and gets rejected back into the range. That suggests buy-side liquidity above the highs may be getting absorbed while short exposure is built.

---

## Required Conditions
- Price is trading inside a defined range
- Price repeatedly fakeouts above the range high
- Each fakeout fails to hold above the range
- Price rejects back into the range after sweeping upside liquidity
- Breakout volume declines with each push higher
- Downside liquidity builds below higher lows

## Supporting Signals
- 4th or later fakeout above range high
- Higher lows form inside the range, creating visible stop clusters below
- Buy-side liquidity above the range high gets worked repeatedly
- Supply remains in control after each failed breakout
- No strong bottoming structure is visible
- No major catalyst supports a sustainable reversal

## Invalid / Weak Version
- Weak if price accepts above the range high
- Weak if breakout volume expands strongly
- Weak if price retests the range high as support and holds
- Weak if downside liquidity is swept and price reclaims strongly
- Invalid if structure shifts bullish with strong demand and sustained volume

---

## Failure Conditions
- Price breaks and holds above the range high
- Price forms acceptance above the range instead of rejecting
- CVD and spot volume rise strongly on the breakout
- Downside liquidity is swept first and price reclaims with strength
- Economic catalyst changes the market structure

## What Usually Happens Next
Price may reject from the range high and rotate back toward the range low. If higher lows have stacked stops below them, those stops can become sell-side fuel for a sustained move lower once price breaks down.

## What Confirms Entry
- Fakeout above range high
- Rejection back into the range
- Declining volume on the breakout attempt
- Failed reclaim above range high
- Downside liquidity building below higher lows

## What Cancels the Idea
- Clean breakout and acceptance above range high
- Strong rising volume on the breakout
- Range high flips into support
- Downside sweep and reclaim occurs first
- Market catalyst supports reversal
