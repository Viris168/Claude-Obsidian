# Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD)

## Definition
The running total of buying volume minus selling volume over time. Shows who is in control — buyers or sellers.

## Reading It
- Rising CVD + rising price = healthy bullish move
- Falling CVD + rising price = divergence, weak buyers (potential reversal)
- Rising CVD + falling price = divergence, absorption (potential reversal up)

## Key Signals
- CVD divergence at key levels = high-probability reversal signal
- CVD confirmation on breakout = continuation likely
