## Recurring Themes

Update this section as patterns in lessons emerge.

### Sweep risk
- Heavy downside liquidity is a magnet even in bullish trends
- Sweep + reclaim is a stronger confirmation than no sweep at all

### Positioning reads
- Low TRL + high OI delta supports continuation but is not a confirmed trigger alone
- Always combine with structure, CVD, and price behavior before acting

### Confidence calibration
- Missing fields (funding, CVD, volume) should lower confidence, not be ignored
- A setup with 3 strong signals beats a setup with 8 weak signals
