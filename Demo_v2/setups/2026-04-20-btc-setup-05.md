## AI Analysis

BTC is showing repeated failed attempts above the range high. The 4th fakeout above the range high suggests upside liquidity is being worked, but price has not accepted above the range. Because each push higher is followed by rejection back into the range, the structure currently supports a short re-accumulation read more than a clean bullish reversal.

Strong bottoms after a prolonged downtrend usually take time to form. They often show repeated sweeps of range lows, seller exhaustion, declining volume on each downside sweep, and fast reclaim behavior after sell-side liquidity is absorbed. In this setup, the behavior is inverted: price keeps sweeping or testing the range high, failing to hold above it, and returning back into the range.

The higher lows inside the range are important because they can create stacked downside liquidity. If price breaks those higher lows, the stops below may become market sell orders and help fuel a move toward the range low. The downside thesis remains strongest while breakout volume keeps declining, price fails to accept above range high, and no catalyst appears to support a sustainable reversal.

### Most Likely Path
